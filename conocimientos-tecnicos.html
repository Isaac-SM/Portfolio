Technologies and Tools

Technical skills built and validated through academic, personal, and professional projects.

Programming and Software

  • Python for robotics, signal processing, computer vision, and rapid prototyping.
  • C and C++ for firmware, algorithms, and localization and navigation systems.
  • MATLAB for analysis, modeling, and academic engineering work.
  • ROS and ROS 2 for node-based architectures, sensor integration, navigation, and control.
  • Basic experience with automation and PLCs in a university setting using TwinCAT.
  • Arduino, VHDL, RAPID, and CubeIDE as part of different development and integration environments.

Robotics and Integration

  • Autonomous navigation with A*, extended Kalman filters, and reactive strategies such as APF.
  • Visual odometry with RGBD, feature extraction and tracking, and robust estimation with RANSAC.
  • Integration of manipulators, grippers, microcontrollers, and serial communication.
  • Work with social robots, robotic arms, mobile platforms, and HRI systems.
  • Prototyping, experimental validation, and troubleshooting in real systems.

Hardware, Design, and Fabrication

  • CAD design with Fusion 360 and AutoCAD.
  • PCB design, soldering, and electronic prototyping.
  • Mechanical design for 3D printing and functional fabrication.
  • Component selection, thermal integration, and maintainability analysis.
  • Hands-on experience repairing and optimizing electronic devices and digital fabrication equipment.

Skills Applied in Projects

The projects in the portfolio show these capabilities in concrete contexts.

From Embedded Hardware to Fabrication

In projects such as the macro pad and the high-temperature 3D printer, I worked on CAD design, PCB design, firmware, microcontrollers, electronics, 3D printing, and mechanical design driven by real requirements.

Perception, Navigation, and Autonomy

In Turf Tank and Mirokai, I developed solutions using ROS, ROS 2, RGBD, visual odometry, global planning, obstacle avoidance, and social navigation for robots operating in dynamic environments.

Human-Robot Interaction

In NeuroSteadyControl and in my master's thesis, I worked with BCI, EEG, signal processing, computer vision, deep learning, and engagement detection to improve how robots understand and interact with people.

Real-World Systems Integration

As a student assistant, I integrated grippers, UR robots, STM32 microcontrollers, and communication layers in projects where coordination between hardware and software is critical for robust system performance. This is complemented by basic experience in automation and PLCs with TwinCAT acquired during my university education.
