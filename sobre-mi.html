About Me

I am Isaac Sbai Metaich, an engineer with a background in electronics, robotics, and mechatronics, currently specializing in robotics through my master's degree. I am interested in building systems that combine hardware, software, and intelligence so robots can be useful, robust, and natural in real environments.

How I Would Describe Myself

I am a curious and creative person with a strong learn-by-doing mindset. I enjoy understanding how things work internally, taking them apart, improving them, or building a new version from scratch if needed. That approach has allowed me to move comfortably across electronics, programming, mechanical design, fabrication, and robotics.

I feel especially comfortable in projects that require connecting multiple disciplines: integrating sensors, solving communication issues between systems, building functional prototypes, or turning an idea into a tangible solution. I also place a lot of value on explaining technical work clearly, because a good solution should not only work, it should also be understandable and maintainable.

What I Bring

  • The ability to move from concept to functional prototype with a very practical mindset.
  • A cross-disciplinary view across hardware, software, integration, and validation.
  • The ability to learn new technologies quickly and apply them in real projects.
  • Creativity to find solutions when the direct path does not work.

How I Work

  • I work well in teams and adapt easily to multidisciplinary environments.
  • I take troubleshooting and iterative improvement of real systems seriously.
  • I try to maintain a balance between technical rigor, initiative, and execution.
  • I enjoy collaborating on projects where technology serves a clear and measurable purpose.
